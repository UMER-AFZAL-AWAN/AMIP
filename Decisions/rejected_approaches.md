# Rejected Approaches
- **Monolithic Architecture**: Rejected due to scaling concerns and mixing AI/Python dependencies with traditional web backend logic.
- **TensorFlow**: Rejected in favor of PyTorch due to team familiarity and dynamic computation graph advantages.
- **MongoDB**: Rejected. Relational integrity is required for user and financial market data.
- **Angular**: Rejected. Team prefers React's ecosystem and Vite's faster build times.
