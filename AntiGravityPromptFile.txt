## Data Integrity

Check:

* Invalid prices.

---

## Survivorship Bias

Do not train using information unavailable at prediction time.

---

## Look-Ahead Bias

Never allow future information into training features.

Example:

Wrong:

Using today's high to predict today's opening.

Correct:

Only use information available before prediction time.

A model is not valuable because it produces predictions.

* Robust across different market conditions.
* Stable over time.
